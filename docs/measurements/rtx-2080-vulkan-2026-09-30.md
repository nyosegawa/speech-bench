# Speech recognition and synthesis on an RTX 2080 with Vulkan, 2026-09-30

The Windows test machine: Windows 11 (build 26200), Core i9-9900K, 32 GB, GeForce RTX 2080 with 8 GB and
driver 591.86, with nothing else on the GPU but the desktop (1.7 GB). The same inputs, runtimes and
preparation as on the Apple M5 (asr-on-an-apple-m5-2026-09-30.md, tts-on-an-apple-m5-2026-09-30.md), with
Vulkan instead of Metal and CrispASR's language detection off.

## Speech recognition, first 100 FLEURS test utterances, cut like ASIST

| Locale | Model | Error rate | Median wait | p90 wait | Median wait on the M5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ja-JP | Qwen3-ASR 1.7B | 5.48% | 0.37 s | 0.56 s | 0.70 s |
| ja-JP | parakeet-tdt_ctc-0.6b-ja | 8.18% | 0.44 s | 0.74 s | 0.18 s |
| ja-JP | ReazonSpeech NeMo v2 | 7.28% | 0.49 s | 0.88 s | 0.21 s |
| en-US | Qwen3-ASR 1.7B | 4.18% | 0.30 s | 0.43 s | 0.58 s |
| en-US | parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3 | 5.74% | 0.30 s | 0.44 s | 0.12 s |

- On the RTX 2080 the CrispASR models wait as long as Qwen3-ASR 1.7B or longer, while on the M5 they wait
  a quarter of it. The model is not what is slow: CrispASR's Vulkan path runs little of the FastConformer
  on the GPU. Its command line transcribed a 10.4 s utterance with parakeet-ja in 1.5 to 1.8 s with the
  GPU and 2.2 to 2.3 s without it, model loading included.
- The error rates of parakeet and ReazonSpeech are the same as on the M5 to within 0.04 points; Qwen3-ASR's
  Japanese is 0.17 points higher, within what the GPU's arithmetic moves.

## Speech synthesis, the 20 Japanese sentences, heard by Qwen3-ASR 1.7B

| Model | Heard CER | Median first audio | p90 first audio | RTF | Median first audio on the M5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3-TTS 0.6B | 12.77% | 0.034 s | 0.045 s | 0.34 | 0.050 s |
| Qwen3-TTS 1.7B | 11.94% | 0.043 s | 0.060 s | 0.36 | 0.077 s |
| Irodori-TTS v4 Small, 8 steps | 1.99% | 0.67 s | 1.47 s | 0.23 | 1.04 s |
| Irodori-TTS v4 Small, 16 steps | 1.82% | 0.80 s | 1.53 s | 0.16 | 1.22 s |
| Irodori-TTS v4 Small, 40 steps | 2.16% | 1.23 s | 2.37 s | 0.24 | 1.89 s |

- Irodori-TTS runs faster on the RTX 2080 than on the M5, and fits in its 8 GB: the long sentences reached
  their first audio in about 1.5 s at 8 steps, against 3.0 to 3.5 s on the M5.
- Qwen3-TTS samples its speech, so the same sentence reads differently from run to run. On both machines
  it misread 暗証番号 and 8264 in some runs, repeated うん, and added words after a sentence (今は一ドルが百四十
  八円三十二銭です。シルエットコーナーアナス on the RTX 2080). One reading of 20 sentences cannot tell the two
  machines apart; the heard CER of Qwen3-TTS moved from 4% to 12% between runs of the same model.
